Refund Policy
-------------
Customers can request a refund within 30 days of purchase if the product has not been damaged due to misuse.
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after approval.

Shipping Policy
---------------
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days.
Express shipping takes 1-2 business days.
Customers receive a tracking ID once the order is shipped.

Payment Issues
--------------
If a payment is deducted but the order is not confirmed, customers should wait 30 minutes.
If the order is still not confirmed, contact customer support with the transaction ID.
Refunds for failed payments are generally processed within 5 business days.

Password Recovery
-----------------
Customers can reset their password using the "Forgot Password" option on the login page.
A password reset link is sent to the registered email address.

Order Cancellation
------------------
Orders can be cancelled before they are shipped.
After shipment, orders cannot be cancelled but may be returned according to the refund policy.
